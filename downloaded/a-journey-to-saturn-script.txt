Title: A Journey to Saturn
HERO: Captain's log, day forty-one: our ship left the orbital station before dawn, carrying one crew, one map, and a promise to reach Saturn.
HERO: Behind us, Earth became a blue lantern in the dark, while the station ring turned slowly and its windows blinked like patient stars.
HERO: We crossed a river of asteroids, each stone older than every ocean on Earth, and listened to the hull sing in the cold.
HERO: Then mission control faded into static, and for one minute, the only voice aboard was my own heartbeat against the metal walls.
HERO: I followed the rescue beacon beyond the shadow of a broken moon, where drifting ice caught sunlight and opened like a silver road.
HERO: At last Saturn rose before us, gold and violet, wearing rings so wide that our ship seemed smaller than a grain of dust.
HERO: We sailed above those rings, over rivers of ice and stone, and every instrument fell silent as if the ship understood wonder.
HERO: This is Captain Aris, sending our signal home: we came looking for a planet, but between the stars, we found our courage.
