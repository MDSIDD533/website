Title: The Last Survivor (Jungle War) — Four-Shot Movie Cut

Production direction: Four separately rendered 15-second Blender shots at 1280x720/15fps with the Ultra Fast profile, joined by FFmpeg into one 60-second film. Shot 1 is a wide rear establishing shot of the lone hero walking past wreckage and memorials. Shot 2 cuts to a telephoto enemy-camp POV from behind crouched enemy silhouettes, watching the hero from his rear-left. Shot 3 is a tight camera behind the hero's helmet, wet poncho, radio pack and slung rifle. Shot 4 is the ambush/action shot with muzzle flashes, tracers, blasts and a pull-back toward the stream. The hero's face is never shown. Piper Joe provides the Hero voice, with locally generated rain, distant gunfire and restrained cinematic music mixed under the dialogue.

HERO: Rain was falling over the broken ridge when my last radio went silent, and in that stillness I understood that my unit was gone.
HERO: I am the only soldier still standing in this drowned valley, yet every fallen brother marches beside me through the darkness and mud.
HERO: Somewhere beyond the burnt camp, enemy eyes were searching the trail, waiting for hunger, fear, or one careless sound to betray me.
HERO: They believed the jungle had swallowed the last of us, but every silent step carried me closer to the place where their trap would fail.
HERO: I carry their dog tags against my heart, cold as river stones, and every name gives my legs another reason to keep moving forward.
HERO: My boots are filled with rain, my compass is cracked, and the flooded trail has vanished beneath the water, but I remember the way home.
HERO: Then the treeline flashed, the rain filled with fire, and I pressed into the mud with their names beating harder than my heart.
HERO: So before dawn breaks beyond the ridge, I will rise with their courage beside me, carry their names through this jungle, tell their story, and walk out of this war alive.
